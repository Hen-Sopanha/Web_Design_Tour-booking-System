Thoroughly Explore Kampot Province on a 4-Days Tour

Kampot 4 Days, 3 Nights Adventure, Mountain, Culture 4.8 (120 Reviews)
×
  • Take in sweeping views from Bokor Mountain.
  • Taste the world-famous Kampot pepper on a farm tour.
  • Explore the lush Kampot countryside, dotted with rice fields, rivers, and hidden villages.

Start your Kampot adventure in the riverside town, wandering its charming streets and riverside cafés.

Visit Bokor Mountain for misty peaks and panoramic views, then explore the peaceful countryside with rice fields and local villages.

Stop at La Plantation to learn about Kampot’s famous pepper and taste its unique flavors.

End the trip at Phnom Chhngok Cave Temple, a centuries-old limestone cave with ancient carvings and fascinating history.

Day 1 - Meetup & Kampot Province
  • 06:30 AM: Meet at the assigned meetup point
  • 10:00 AM: Arrive in Kampot Province and check in to your hotel.
  • 11:30 AM: Take a leisurely walk through the town's charming streets.
  • 03:00 PM: Enjoy riverside cafes and the relaxed town atmosphere.
Day 2- Bokor Mountain Excursion
  • 08:30 AM: Drive to Bokor Mountain in Kampot Province for cool highland air and panoramic views.
  • 10:00 AM: Explore the abandoned French hill station and colonial-era buildings.
  • 12:00 PM: Stop at viewpoints for photos of the coastline and surrounding landscapes and have lunch.
  • 05:00 PM: Return to Kampot Town for dinner and relaxation.
Day 3 - Kampot Countryside & La Plantation
  • 08:30 AM: Explore the Kampot countryside, passing rice fields, rivers, and local villages.
  • 10:30 AM: Visit La Plantation to learn about Kampot pepper farming and taste its unique flavors.
  • 12:00 PM: Optional stops at local markets or riverside spots along the way.
  • 05:00 PM: Return to Kampot Town for a relaxing evening.
Day 4 - Phnom Chhngok Cave Temple & Departure
  • 08:30 AM: Visit Phnom Chhngok Cave Temple, an ancient limestone cave with historical carvings.
  • 11:30 AM: Enjoy a riverside lunch in Kampot Town.
  • 4:00 PM: Get ready for return trip back to Phnom Penh.
